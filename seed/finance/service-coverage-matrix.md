# Service Coverage Matrix

Give a book-wide view of which services each relationship uses, so white space and
over-concentration are obvious at a glance.

## When to use

- Portfolio / book reviews across all relationships.
- Setting cross-sell priorities at the team level.
- Spotting concentration risk in the revenue base.

## Inputs

- The set of relationships (with [[family-office-profile]] where available).
- The service catalogue.
- Which services are active per relationship.

## Steps

1. Build a grid: rows = relationships, columns = services, cells = active / eligible / n/a.
2. Mark eligible-but-unused cells as white space (the cross-sell opportunity surface).
3. Summarise coverage per service and per relationship.
4. Highlight concentration — services or clients that dominate the revenue base.
5. Rank the highest-value white-space cells for follow-up via [[cross-sell-recommender]].

## Output

A coverage matrix, per-service and per-client coverage summaries, and a ranked white-space list.
