# Multi-Jurisdiction Tax Filing

Keep a family-office structure's filing obligations, deadlines and information-reporting
readiness organised across every jurisdiction it touches.

## When to use

- Compiling the annual filing calendar for a multi-entity structure.
- Checking FATCA/CRS and W-8/W-9 readiness before a deadline or an account opening.
- Onboarding a new entity into the compliance workflow.

## Inputs

- The legal entities and their jurisdictions of incorporation and tax residence.
- Filing types applicable to each entity (returns, information reporting, registrations).
- Known deadlines and the responsible party for each.

## Steps

1. List every entity with its jurisdictions and applicable filing types.
2. Attach the deadline and owner to each obligation; build a consolidated calendar.
3. Assess FATCA/CRS classification and required self-certifications per entity.
4. Track W-8/W-9 status for accounts and counterparties.
5. Flag missing documents, approaching deadlines and any classification gaps.

## Output

A consolidated filing calendar, a per-entity obligation register, and a readiness checklist
for information reporting. Not tax advice — surface obligations for a qualified adviser to confirm.


## Presenting results
- Present every result as one or more clear Markdown **tables** — one per section, each with a short heading.
- Keep prose minimal; put the substance in the tables.
- Offer the user a downloadable **PDF** (formatted) and **CSV** (the underlying rows), and generate them when asked.
- Never invent figures. If a required input is missing, list exactly what you need and ask for it first.
