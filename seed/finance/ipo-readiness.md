# IPO Readiness Assessment

Evaluate whether a company is ready to list and produce a concrete gap list and timeline to
get there.

## When to use

- Considering an IPO vs. private alternatives.
- Planning a listing 12–24 months out and scoping the work.

## Inputs

- Financial history and the ability to report on a public cadence.
- Governance, board composition and internal controls.
- The equity story and comparable public companies.

## Steps

1. Test financial readiness: audit status, track record, forecasting reliability, controls.
2. Review governance: board independence, committees, policies, reporting maturity.
3. Pressure-test the equity story against public comparables and likely investor questions.
4. Map the process: advisers, workstreams, regulatory steps, indicative timeline.
5. Produce a prioritised gap list with owners and lead times.

## Output

A readiness scorecard by dimension, a prioritised remediation gap list, and an indicative
timeline to listing.
