Anthropic open-sourced the Agent Skills format, ChatGPT has Custom GPTs, and every good operator has a notes file full of "prompts that actually work." But the best prompts are trapped — locked inside one app, one repo, or one person's head — so everyone keeps rebuilding the same thing.

It always starts with a (small) frustration — I kept watching sharp people paste the same carefully-built prompt into a fresh chat, tweak it, lose it, and start from scratch next week. So we built FastSkills: an open catalogue of reusable skills you can take to any LLM. BYOS — Bring Your Own Skills.

A few things FastSkills does differently: 👇

🧩 One skill, any model — every skill is a plain SKILL.md. Open it in Claude, ChatGPT or Grok in one click. Your skills shouldn't be married to one vendor.

🔓 Open, not locked in — browse and download 180+ skills across Finance, Trading, Legal and Marketing, MIT/Apache-licensed where the source allows. Read the instructions, don't just trust a black box.

⑂ Clone like code — fork any skill into your own editable copy in one click, adapt the wording, examples and steps, and keep your version. Attribution to the original stays intact.

🕒 Versioned prompts — the library is the default version, maintained in GitHub; your edits are versioned in your own account. Roll back any time.

🔐 Public by default, private when you need it — publish a skill to share it, or keep an internal one to yourself and your team.

📊 Answers as reports, not walls of text — every Finance and Trading skill returns clean tables plus a downloadable PDF and CSV. Deal triage, credit screening, comps, rent-roll parsing, SEO audits, backtests — as something you can actually hand to a colleague.

⭐ Build a shelf — bookmark the skills you rely on, and contribute the ones you make. Sharing is caring: the catalogue gets better every time someone gives one back.

If you work in Finance, Trading, Legal or Marketing — try a skill on a real task this week and tell me where it falls short. If you're a builder, fork one, improve it, and publish it back.
